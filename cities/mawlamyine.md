Sitting where the Thanlwin River meets the sea in Mon State, Mawlamyine offers relaxed waterfront views, ridge-top pagodas, and distinct local flavors.

## Top Things to Do

- **Watch Sunset from the Ridge:** Look out over the river mouth and surrounding hills from hilltop pagodas.
- **Explore Mon Specialties:** Try local Mon dishes, snacks, and coastal seafood.
- **Take a Riverfront Walk:** Enjoy breezy evenings along the water.
