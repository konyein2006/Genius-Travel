Bagan is home to one of the world's richest concentrations of historic Buddhist monuments set across a dry, scenic plain along the Ayeyarwady River.

## Top Things to Do

- **Sunrise & Sunset Views:** Watch early morning mist and golden hour light across the temple plain.
- **Explore Architecture:** Visit classic temples and stupas in Old Bagan and Nyaung-U.
- **Try Lacquerware Craft Tours:** See how traditional Bagan lacquerware is woven, painted, and polished by hand.
