Mandalay is Myanmar's second-largest city and the last royal capital before British rule. Sitting along the Ayeyarwady River, it serves as a gateway to ancient surrounding capitals like Amarapura, Sagaing, and Mingun.

## Top Things to Do

- **Sunset at U Bein Bridge:** Walk across the 1.2 km teakwood bridge over Taungthaman Lake in Amarapura.
- **Climb Mandalay Hill:** Take in panoramic views of the city and surrounding plains.
- **Explore Local Crafts:** Visit workshops known for gold-leaf beating, woodcarving, and marionettes.

## Local Food to Try

- **Mandalay Meeshay:** Rice noodles served with rich meat sauce, pickled mustard greens, and roasted chickpea flour.
