Perched high above the surrounding valley in Shan State, Taunggyi combines crisp mountain air with bustling local markets and regional traditions.

## Top Things to Do

- **Explore Local Markets:** Wander through stalls filled with seasonal fruits, flowers, and Shan specialties.
- **Take in Highland Views:** Enjoy panoramic overlooks across the surrounding Shan plateau.
- **Taste Regional Favorites:** Enjoy warm noodle soups, grilled skewers, and local coffee.
