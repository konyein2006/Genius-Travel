Set in the Shan Hills at a higher elevation than Mandalay, Pyin Oo Lwin offers crisp air, lush greenery, and a slower pace.

## Top Things to Do

- **Walk the Botanical Gardens:** Explore flower beds, shady paths, and tree collections.
- **Visit Nearby Waterfalls:** Spend a cool afternoon around local cascades and scenic viewpoints.
- **Enjoy Local Treats:** Taste fresh strawberries, local coffee, and highland snacks.
