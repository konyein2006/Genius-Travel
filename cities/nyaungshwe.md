Located at the northern tip of Inle Lake, Nyaungshwe makes a convenient and welcoming base for exploring lake life and surrounding Shan hills.

## Top Things to Do

- **Take a Boat Ride on Inle Lake:** Glide past stilt houses, floating gardens, and traditional fishing boats.
- **Cycle or Walk Around Town:** Ride past rice paddies, monasteries, and nearby vineyards.
- **Sample Shan Cuisine:** Try Shan noodles, tofu nway, and local tea-leaf dishes.
