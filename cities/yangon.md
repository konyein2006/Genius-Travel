Yangon is Myanmar's largest city and main international gateway, offering a mix of gilded stupas, historic neighborhoods, and busy night markets.

## Top Things to Do

- **Visit Shwedagon Pagoda:** Experience the iconic golden stupa at dusk when the lights glow over the city.
- **Stroll Around Kandawgyi Lake:** Enjoy peaceful boardwalk views and morning or evening walks.
- **Explore Street Food:** Sample mohinga, samosa salad, and tea-shop snacks across downtown streets.
