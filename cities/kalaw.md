Tucked into the hills of southern Shan State, Kalaw is a favorite starting point for multi-day or day hikes through rural villages and terraced farmland.

## Top Things to Do

- **Go on a Guided Trek:** Hike through pine woods, hill ridges, and farming communities.
- **Browse Kalaw Market:** Pick up local produce, Shan snacks, and handmade goods.
- **Relax in the Cool Air:** Enjoy quiet mornings and cozy meals after a day outdoors.
